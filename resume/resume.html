BBVA Peru needed to evolve its core banking platform to enable new digital products without disrupting critical operations. I led the strategy for that evolution: 11 of the new Core's 16 reusable capabilities implemented across data, banking architecture, and multichannel layers, so products are built on top instead of rebuilding customer, consent, or signature management each time.

  • Enabled the Retail transformation (access, consents, global position, transaction history in the mobile app) and Business banking processes (mass payroll payments, multi-signature approvals, business mobile app).
