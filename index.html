Leadership Case Study

Leading the Core Banking transformation

Role: Product Owner → Sponsor Global program: Horizon Alpha (Holding-funded, >€1M over 3 years) Where: BBVA Peru When: 2019–2024

BBVA Peru needed to evolve its core banking platform to launch new digital products and services without adding obsolescence cost or risk to critical operations. I led the strategy for that evolution, and we implemented 11 of the new Core's 16 reusable capabilities across three layers — data, banking architecture, and multichannel — so each product is built on top instead of rebuilding customer, consent, or signature management every time. On that Core we enabled the Retail transformation (access management, consents, global position, and transaction history in the mobile app) and Business banking processes (mass payroll payments, multi-signature approvals, and the evolution of the business mobile app).

  • As Product Owner of Horizon Alpha, I accelerated the implementation of the key modules needed to support new banking products and services.
  • Then, as Sponsor of the global Horizon Alpha program, I drove adoption of the new Core across the Group's and the country's strategic initiatives.
  • Implemented the core banking functional architecture across the bank's technical architectures — services (ASO), backend (APX), middleware (SEMaaS), batch processing (LRABatch), and frontend (Cells Web) — in coordination with BBVA Group's global architecture teams, keeping the system modular and scalable.
  • Mobilized 100+ people across the country and the Holding — security, development, change management, production, architecture, fraud, and the Mexico and Spain holdings — to deploy each Alpha Core release (1,500+ components) in under a quarter, with quality automation. We were the first country in the Group to achieve a successful release with zero production impact.
