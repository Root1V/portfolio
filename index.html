About

Strategy and architecture, not just one or the other

I lead AI & Cloud Architecture strategy at BBVA Peru, at the intersection of business strategy, technology architecture, and innovation — defining how a regulated financial institution adopts agentic AI, GenAI platforms, and cloud-native architecture without compromising security or compliance.

My path — from software developer to IT architect to today's AI & Enterprise Architecture lead — gives me a perspective that combines strategic vision with hands-on implementation judgment: I understand both the business decisions behind an AI initiative and the architectural details that determine whether it actually works in production. Since my first year at BBVA, that's meant working hand-in-hand with the Group's international architecture teams — Spain, Mexico, Colombia, Argentina, Turkey — coordinating standards and solutions across geographies, not just within Peru. Outside my role at BBVA, I maintain a self-hosted lab of production-grade AI infrastructure — the five projects below are a snapshot of that work, all open source, no vendor lock-in.

Leadership Case Study

Leading the Core Banking transformation

Role: Sponsor → Product Owner Program: Horizon Alpha Where: BBVA Peru When: 2019–2024

BBVA Peru needed to evolve its core banking platform to enable new digital products and services without disrupting critical operations. I led the strategy for that evolution, aligning modern technology with the bank's business objectives.

  • Acted as Sponsor of the global Horizon Alpha program, driving adoption of the new Core across the country's strategic initiatives.
  • Later served as Product Owner of Horizon Alpha 1.0, accelerating implementation of the key modules needed to support new banking products and services.
  • Integrated the transactional architecture (APX) with asynchronous messaging platforms, coordinating with BBVA Group's global architecture teams to keep the system modular and scalable.
  • Represented engineering on the bank's Innovation Leadership team, helping structure cross-cutting innovation programs.
